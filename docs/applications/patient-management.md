# Patient Management

## Status

Founder-approved product direction. Patient Management is not implemented as a new application by this document and is not approved for implementation in this PR.

## Product role

Patient Management describes the patient-level continuity surface for organizing patient state, current relevance, unresolved constraints, operational attention, and next implications across time.

Within the current repository, patient-oriented surfaces exist as part of Clinical Continuity and the OT configuration. This document preserves the approved direction while keeping current implementation truth separate.

## Expected concerns

Patient Management direction may involve:

- patient-level current state orientation;
- continuity across visits or episodes;
- unresolved issues and constraints;
- current relevance and attention state;
- evidence-linked patient summaries;
- operational next implications;
- verification and correction workflows.

## Boundaries

This document does not authorize:

- new Patient Management implementation;
- schema, API, persistence, or route changes;
- changes to clinical reasoning, progression, ranking, or continuity logic;
- changes to AI generation behavior;
- conversion of future direction into active roadmap work without explicit approval.
