# Current Focus

## Single active work boundary

# Clinical Continuity Product Design Readiness

## Objective

Prepare the implemented Clinical Continuity application for Product Design by consolidating approved product definition, approved platform and application boundaries, current implemented capabilities, unresolved Founder or Architecture decisions, and constraints Product Design must preserve.

This boundary follows completion of the Shared Continuity Foundation validation and promotion boundary. The Shared Continuity Foundation is now approved only as conceptual platform responsibility ownership, not as a shared implementation.

## Included scope

- consolidate approved Clinical Continuity product definition;
- consolidate approved platform, Shared Continuity Foundation, Clinical Continuity, OT configuration, Care Continuity, and delivery-infrastructure boundaries;
- summarize current implemented Clinical Continuity capabilities from repository evidence;
- identify unresolved Founder or Architecture decisions that Product Design must not silently answer;
- identify constraints Product Design must preserve, including deterministic authority, current-versus-historical truth separation, evidence lineage, human verification, and AI synthesis boundaries;
- prepare a Product Design readiness packet only.

## Excluded scope

- Product Design itself;
- information architecture;
- navigation;
- screens;
- workflows;
- interaction models;
- UI implementation;
- runtime implementation;
- shared foundation implementation or extraction;
- Care Continuity implementation;
- Patient Management implementation;
- engine refactoring;
- schema redesign;
- API redesign;
- persistence redesign;
- clinical logic changes;
- progression logic changes;
- reassessment logic changes;
- continuity logic changes;
- operational prioritization changes;
- AI prompt or generation behavior changes;
- PT, SLP, or other discipline expansion.

## Completion criteria

The boundary is complete when Product Design has one authoritative readiness reference that clearly distinguishes:

- approved product definition;
- approved platform and application boundaries;
- current implemented capabilities;
- deferred or unresolved decisions;
- constraints Product Design must preserve;
- what Product Design is not authorized to decide in this boundary.

## Stopping condition

Stop before Product Design, implementation, refactoring, technical extraction, UX design, IA design, navigation design, screen design, workflow design, interaction design, or runtime changes.
