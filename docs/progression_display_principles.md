# Progression Display Principles

---

## Purpose
The progression and operational prioritization display exists to support:
- rapid clinician orientation
- continuity visibility
- operational prioritization
- longitudinal workflow cohesion
- reassessment readiness
- clear current treatment emphasis

The system does NOT exist to:
- generate motivational rehabilitation narratives
- visualize speculative recovery trajectories
- gamify rehabilitation progression
- replace clinical judgment
- increase documentation burden
- present competing AI-generated treatment philosophies

---

## Core Display Model
The display should organize information around this hierarchy:

1. Current Progression State
2. Current Operational Emphasis
3. Dominant Barriers
4. Adjacent Operational Priorities
5. Reassessment Triggers
6. Continuity Summary

This replaces the older display assumption that selected pathway or alternative treatment approaches are the primary organizing model.

---

## Core Principles
- progression should reinforce workflow clarity
- progression should remain cognitively compressed
- progression should prioritize operational barriers over abstract status
- progression should support current operational emphasis authority
- progression should avoid timeline-heavy visualization initially
- progression should avoid dense longitudinal narratives
- progression should favor explainable deterministic logic
- progression should surface actionable continuity signals only
- adjacent priorities should not be presented as competing treatment plans
- operational emphasis should clarify what dominates treatment attention right now

---

## Progression State vs Operational Emphasis

### Progression State
Describes the current operational continuity condition.

Example:
- foundational participation

### Operational Emphasis
Describes the dominant intervention priority right now.

Example:
- caregiver-supported task setup

These should be displayed as related but distinct concepts.

Do not visually imply that progression phase and operational emphasis must always match exactly.

---

## Adjacent Operational Priorities
Adjacent priorities replace “Alternative Treatment Approaches.”

They should represent:
- nearby emphasis areas
- secondary operational concerns
- emerging readiness areas
- barriers to monitor
- possible future emphasis shifts after reassessment

They should not be framed as:
- optional competing plans
- alternate treatment philosophies
- mutually exclusive strategies
- clinician-selected AI pathways

---

## Initial UX Constraints
The initial display should:
- remain lightweight
- avoid deep nesting
- avoid large expandable sections
- avoid excessive milestone counts
- avoid visual clutter
- prioritize scanability over detail richness
- make current operational emphasis more visually prominent than adjacent priorities
- keep reassessment triggers visible only when clinically relevant

---

## Recommended Display Pattern

### Primary Orientation Area
Show:
- Current Progression State chip
- Current Operational Emphasis label
- 1–2 sentence continuity summary

### Operational Priority Area
Show:
- dominant barriers
- safety risks
- caregiver feasibility issues
- environmental constraints

### Adjacent Priority Area
Show:
- secondary priorities only after the current emphasis is clear
- concise labels with short rationale
- no selection affordance unless the product intentionally supports a reassessment/change workflow

### Reassessment Area
Show:
- reassessment triggers
- stale-state warnings
- follow-up change prompts

---

## Visual Hierarchy Rule
Current operational emphasis should be visually dominant.

Adjacent priorities should be visually secondary.

Historical snapshots should be clearly distinguished from the live operational case state.

---

## Copy and Labeling Guidance

Prefer:
- Current Operational Emphasis
- Why This Matters Now
- Dominant Barriers
- Adjacent Operational Priorities
- Reassessment Triggers
- Continuity Summary

Avoid:
- Alternative Treatment Approaches
- Choose a Pathway
- Competing Plans
- Recommended Pathway vs Alternative Pathways
- AI Treatment Options

---

## Deferred UX Concepts
The following are intentionally deferred:
- progression timelines
- comparative visit graphs
- trajectory forecasting
- predictive recovery indicators
- gamified progression metaphors
- automated progression scoring
- large longitudinal dashboards
- multi-visit trend visualizations
- pathway comparison dashboards

These may be reconsidered later only if they improve:
- clinician orientation
- operational usability
- continuity clarity

without increasing:
- cognitive burden
- workflow fragmentation
- interpretive ambiguity
