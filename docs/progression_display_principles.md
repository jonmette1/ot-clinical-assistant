# Progression Display Principles

## Purpose

The progression system exists to support:
- rapid clinician orientation
- continuity visibility
- operational prioritization
- longitudinal workflow cohesion

The system does NOT exist to:
- generate motivational rehabilitation narratives
- visualize speculative recovery trajectories
- gamify rehabilitation progression
- replace clinical judgment
- increase documentation burden

---

## Core Principles

- progression should reinforce workflow clarity
- progression should remain cognitively compressed
- progression should prioritize operational barriers over abstract status
- progression should remain secondary to selected pathway authority
- progression should avoid timeline-heavy visualization initially
- progression should avoid dense longitudinal narratives
- progression should favor explainable deterministic logic
- progression should surface actionable continuity signals only

---

## Initial UX Constraints

The initial progression display should:
- remain lightweight
- avoid deep nesting
- avoid large expandable sections
- avoid excessive milestone counts
- avoid visual clutter
- prioritize scanability over detail richness

---

## Deferred UX Concepts

The following are intentionally deferred:
- progression timelines
- comparative visit graphs
- trajectory forecasting
- predictive recovery indicators
- gamified progression metaphors
- automated progression scoring
- large longitudinal dashboards
- multi-visit trend visualizations

These may be reconsidered later only if they improve:
- clinician orientation
- operational usability
- continuity clarity

without increasing:
- cognitive burden
- workflow fragmentation
- interpretive ambiguity
