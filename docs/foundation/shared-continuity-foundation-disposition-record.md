# Shared Continuity Foundation Candidate Disposition Record

## Status

This record documents the disposition of every evaluated candidate capability for the Shared Continuity Foundation promotion boundary. It is a governance and architecture-synthesis artifact only. It does not authorize implementation, extraction, schemas, APIs, persistence, UI, workflows, or Care Continuity build work.

Allowed dispositions are: Approved Shared Foundation, Deferred, Clinical Continuity, Care Continuity, Discipline Configuration, and Delivery Infrastructure.
