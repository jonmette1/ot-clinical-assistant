# Clinical Assistant Platform — Platform North Star

Last updated: 2026-06-04

---

## Platform Thesis

The OT Clinical Assistant is the first discipline-specific implementation of a broader rehabilitation-focused Clinical Assistant Platform.

The platform should be understood as a **Clinical Attention System**.

Its purpose is to help clinicians convert fragmented clinical, environmental, caregiver, and longitudinal information into focused attention guidance.

---

## Core Platform Value

The platform creates value through:

* cognitive compression
* rapid orientation
* continuity awareness
* change detection
* prioritization
* clinician attention management

The platform should help a clinician answer:

1. What is happening now?
2. What changed?
3. Why does that matter?
4. What requires attention?
5. What should happen next?

---

## What the Platform Is Not

The platform is not intended to become:

* an EMR
* a documentation replacement
* an analytics dashboard
* a reporting platform
* an autonomous clinician

It may support documentation-adjacent workflows, but documentation production is not the core product identity.

It may preserve longitudinal information, but analytics and reporting are not the current direction.

---

## Discipline Expansion Direction

Current discipline:

* Occupational therapy adult rehabilitation / home health MVP

Post-MVP expansion research:

* PT platform configuration validation
* SLP feasibility review

Expansion should be configuration- and discipline-validation-led. Do not assume that OT fields, reasoning labels, progression signals, or intervention guidance transfer directly.

---

## Longitudinal Direction

Aligned future opportunity:

* **Longitudinal Visibility / Continuity Compression**

This means helping clinicians understand:

* what changed
* why it changed
* what should be done differently
* what transition is approaching
* what treatment implication follows

Not current direction:

* **Longitudinal Intelligence / Analytics**

Avoid dashboard-heavy analytics, reporting surfaces, autonomous predictions, or population-level intelligence as the next product identity.

---

## Platform Guardrails

The platform should preserve:

* deterministic reasoning authority
* clinician-facing language
* live-state versus historical-snapshot safety
* continuity-aware workflows
* discipline-specific validation
* minimal cognitive burden

The platform should avoid:

* exposing internal reasoning architecture as primary UI
* turning historical context into competing current truth
* broad schema/API changes without explicit approval
* expanding faster than clinical validation supports
